About me

Hi, I'm Josh. I'm a 30-something dad living in a small town in Australia, and I make games in my spare time.

Josh Makes Games is just me. I design, build, test and release everything myself, from the art and code to the servers and the store listings. I make cozy couch games: the kind you can play on your phone from the sofa, a few relaxing minutes at a time.

Cozy Acres is my first game. There will be more, in all sorts of styles, made with the same care.
