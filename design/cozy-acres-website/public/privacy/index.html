Who we are

Cozy Acres is made by Josh Makes Games ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Cozy Acres game in your web browser at cozyacres.joshmakesgames92.com, the Cozy Acres Android app on Google Play, and this website.

What we collect

Stored on your device

Your farm, crops, animals, coins, gems and settings are saved on your device (in your browser's storage or in the app's storage). We cannot see this data unless you use online features.

When you use online features

  • Your friend code, farm name and farm level.
  • Your friends list, gifts you send and receive, and items you list or buy in the Shared Market.
  • Leaderboard scores (for example, farm size and charm).
  • [Confirm: any technical data your server stores, such as IP address in server logs, and for how long.]

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location in the game.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases, so no payment details.
  • No selling or renting of personal information.

How we use it

Only to run the game's online features: showing your farm to friends, delivering gifts, running the Shared Market and leaderboards, keeping things fair, and fixing problems.

Sharing

Your farm name, level and leaderboard position are visible to other players. Online data is stored with our hosting provider, [HOSTING PROVIDER], who processes it only on our behalf. We may share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep online data while you use the online features. [Confirm: e.g. data for farms inactive for 12 months is deleted automatically.] When you delete your online data, it is removed from our server within [30] days.

Your choices

  • Delete your online data: use the Delete my data page with your friend code.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the app, or clear site data for cozyacres.joshmakesgames92.com in your browser.
  • Ask a question or make a request: email us (below). Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your information.

Children

Cozy Acres is friendly for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the game data described above, and the game does not ask for it. A parent or guardian can delete a child's online data at any time using the friend code. [Review with your Google Play Families and target-audience settings.]

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page and, for important changes, mention it in the game.

Contact

Email support@joshmakesgames92.com.
