Stuck in the mud? Here are the answers to common questions.

Where is my farm saved?

Your farm is saved on your device. In the browser it lives in your browser's storage, so clearing site data for this website in your browser settings will reset it.

Can I play on both my phone and computer?

Yes. Open Settings, then Online play, and tap Sign in with Google. Your farm is backed up to the cloud, and when you sign in with the same Google account on another device (your phone, tablet, computer or the Android app) your farm comes back there. If both devices already have a farm, the game shows them side by side and lets you choose which one to keep; the other one is kept safe in Settings. Without signing in, your farm stays on that one device, but you can still move it with Export save and Import save in Settings.

How do I add a friend?

Open Friends in the game, copy your friend code and share it. Your friend enters it under "Add friend". You can then visit each other's farms and send gifts.

How does the Shared Market work?

List goods you have made at a price you choose. Other farmers can buy them with coins, and you collect your coins from your market stall.

When do the leaderboards reset?

The This week leaderboard starts fresh every Monday at 00:00 UTC, so everyone has the same week to earn XP. The Level, Farm value and Charm leaderboards never reset: they always show where every farm stands right now.

How do I delete my online data?

In the game, open Settings, then Online play, and tap Delete my online account. Or email us your friend code. See Delete my data for what gets deleted.

I found a bug. What should I send?

Email us your device, browser or Android version, your game version (in Settings) and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.
