Privacy policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026. Last updated: 6 October 2026.

The short version: Cozy Acres does not show ads at the moment, and we do not sell your information. Your farm is saved on your device. If you use the online features (friends, gifts, the Shared Market and leaderboards), a small amount of game data is stored on our server so those features work. If you choose to sign in with Google, a copy of your farm is also kept on our server so you can play it on other devices. You can delete all of it at any time, right in the game.

Who we are

Cozy Acres is made by Josh Makes Games ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Cozy Acres game in your web browser, the Cozy Acres Android app on Google Play, and this website.

What we collect

Stored on your device

Your farm, crops, animals, coins, gems and settings are saved on your device (in your browser's storage or in the app's storage). We cannot see this data unless you use online features.

When you use online features

  • An anonymous account id, created automatically the first time the game goes online. No email or password is needed.
  • Your public profile: friend code, farmer name, farm name (if you set one), your farmer's look (body, colours and hat), level, total XP, farm value, charm and this week's XP.
  • Gifts you send and receive (items, coins and the short note of up to 140 characters you can add) and items you list, sell or buy in the Shared Market.
  • A snapshot of your farm for visits: the layout of your fields, crops, buildings, animals, trees and decorations, with your farmer name, level and look, so neighbours can visit and look around your farm. It contains no coins, gems, items in your barn or anything personal.
  • Neighbour help: when you help a neighbour while visiting (water a field, feed animals, tend a tree) or like their farm, we store who helped whom, what was done, the day, and the guestbook note if you picked one. Guestbook notes are chosen from a short preset list; there is no free text.
  • Play statistics: the days you play, how many times you open the game, roughly how many minutes you play, the game version, and whether you play in the app, an installed web app or a browser. They are never shown to other players.
  • Feedback you send from Settings, Send feedback: your message and its category, with your farmer name, level, game version and your device and browser details (for example "Android 14; Pixel 7"), so we can look into it.
  • Backups: to restore farms if something goes wrong, we keep daily copies of your cloud save (if you signed in with Google) or your farm snapshot for up to 60 days.
  • Technical data: like any website, our hosting providers (GitHub Pages for the website and game files, Supabase for online play) automatically log technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the time of each request to run and protect the service. These logs are kept only for a short time (typically a few days) and we do not use them to identify or track you.

If you sign in with Google (optional)

  • Your email address and Google account id, which Google shares with us when you sign in. We use them only to sign you in. We do not get your Google password, contacts or anything else from your Google account.
  • Your name and profile picture link as provided by Google, if Google includes them.
  • A cloud save: a copy of your farm (the same data as an exported save), the device type it was saved from (for example "Android phone") and when it was saved. Only you can read it.

If you switch on notifications (optional)

If you switch on phone notifications in the game's Settings, we store a push subscription for that device: the address your browser or phone gives us at its push service (run by Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft) and the keys needed to send to it, together with your device's time zone, your quiet hours and which notifications you chose, and the short texts of the reminders planned for the next day (for example "Wheat is ready to harvest"). We use it only to send those notifications. You can turn notifications off at any time in Settings, Notifications (which deletes the subscription from our server), or in your browser or phone settings; deleting your online account also deletes it.

Apart from an optional Google sign-in, we do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts or location in the game.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.
  • No payment details. If optional purchases are added in future, payments are handled by Google Play and we never see your card or bank details.
  • No selling or renting of personal information.

How we use it

Only to run the game's online features: showing your farm to friends and letting neighbours visit and help it, delivering gifts, running the Shared Market and leaderboards, keeping your cloud save if you signed in with Google, restoring farms and sending thank-you or apology gifts, keeping things fair, understanding how the game is played so we can improve it, answering feedback, and fixing problems. We never email you for marketing.

Sharing

Your farmer name, farm name, look, level and leaderboard position are visible to other players, and so is your farm's snapshot when someone visits it. Neighbours you help see your farmer name and any guestbook note you picked. Your email address and cloud save are never shown to other players. The website and game files are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), and online play data (accounts, profiles, gifts, market listings, farm snapshots, neighbour help, notification subscriptions and cloud saves) is stored with Supabase (Supabase, Inc.). Sign in with Google is provided by Google. These providers process data only on our behalf to run the service, and data may be processed in countries other than your own. All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We may share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep online data while you use the online features. Claimed gifts and finished market listings may be cleared out after 30 days. Farm backups are kept for up to 60 days. When you tap Delete my online account in the game, your online data is deleted from our server straight away. If you ask us by email, we delete it within 30 days. Short-lived technical logs held by our hosting providers expire on their own.

Your choices

  • Delete your online account and data: in the game, open Settings, then Online play, and tap Delete my online account. This deletes your sign-in (including your Google email address), profile and friend code, cloud save, farm snapshot, market listings, gifts, neighbour help and likes (both given and received), notification subscriptions, play statistics, feedback and farm backups straight away. The farm on your device stays. You can also email us with your friend code and we will delete it for you.
  • Sign out: Settings, Online play, Sign out stops cloud saving on that device.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the app, or clear site data for this website in your browser settings.
  • Ask a question or make a request: email us (below). Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your information.

Children

Cozy Acres is a gentle game, but it is not directed at children under 13, and its online features (public farmer names, gifts with short notes and the Shared Market) are meant for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the online features, a parent or guardian can delete that online data at any time with the Delete my online account button in the game's Settings, or by emailing us the friend code, and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page and, for important changes, mention it in the game.

If we add advertising or optional purchases in future, we will update this policy first, describe what the ad or payment providers receive, and tell you in the game before they appear.
