  screenshot of Settings > Online play > Sign-in steps, and check Supabase > Authentication > URL Configuration has
  `app.joshmakesgames.cozyacres://auth` and the Auth logs.

- **Gem history** starts with 1.33.6; earlier spends were never recorded.
- **iOS:** never leave the game running in the Simulator: it rendered in software for 45 hours and loaded the Mac so
  much that tests failed (`xcrun simctl terminate <device> app.joshmakesgames.cozyacres` when done).
