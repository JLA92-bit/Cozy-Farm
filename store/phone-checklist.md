# Real-phone test checklist (1.28.5)

For Josh and the testers. Nothing since about 1.18 had been checked on a real phone before this, only in headless
Chromium. Play as normal and tick what you can reach; skip anything your farm hasn't unlocked yet. Write anything odd in
`store/test-log.md` (date, phone, Low/Medium/High, portrait or landscape, what happened, a screenshot if you can).

**Josh: do the whole list on Low, Medium and High** (Settings > Graphics) on the Pixel (rule 9). Testers can stay
on their usual setting, but please say which phone and setting you're on.

## Every phone, first 5 minutes
- [ ] The game opens from the Play app with no browser bar at the top.
- [ ] Tap, drag to move the camera, pinch to zoom: all respond straight away (nothing invisible blocks touches).
- [ ] Turn the phone sideways and back: the farm and the buttons fit both ways.
- [ ] Open and close five or six screens (shop, barn, orders, Book, settings): each fits, scrolls with a finger and
      the red close button can be reached.
- [ ] Leave the game for 10 minutes and come back: it picks up where you were, no white or black screen.

## The farm
- [ ] Place, move and store a few decorations. Nothing vanishes leaving only its shadow (1.20 fix).
- [ ] A busy farm (lots of decor) still feels smooth when you pan around.
- [ ] Farm-edge wildlife: tap a visitor to photograph it (flash, toast, Wildlife Album page in the Book).
- [ ] Farmhouse: the care round, farmhands card (level 50+), Farmhouse upgrades.
- [ ] Vet's Cottage and Carpenter's Workshop (level 10+): treat, repair, check-up.

## The Wild Woods (level 14+, south beach)
- [ ] Walk over, forage, dig. Region bar in the banner.
- [ ] Each restored trail: the footbridge looks whole, the region opens.
- [ ] Critter stumps, Wren's field note, camping in the evening (Woodcraft Lodge level 22+).
- [ ] Night: fireflies show and can be caught. Weather banner reads right.
- [ ] Elder Oak and the Treehouse in Whispering Pines (if reached).

## The village square (east beach jetty)
- [ ] The square loads and the lots look right.
- [ ] Willowmere Manor behind the lots (after the Village Festival).
- [ ] Clock Tower (level 70+): the hands show the real time; it chimes on the hour (sound on).
- [ ] Lighthouse (level 80+): the beam turns at night.

## Inside the Farmhouse (1.29, level 30+)
- [ ] The door above the Farmhouse (or Go inside on the Farmhouse screen) takes you in; Back to the farm brings you out.
- [ ] Tap a piece, then drag it: it follows your finger smoothly; small things climb onto tables and shelves.
- [ ] Turn, Colours, Copy and Paste, Another, Put away and Done all work; Undo and Redo too.
- [ ] Paint: walls (all, left, right, front), lower walls, floor, trim; the room stays in view above the sheet.
- [ ] Turn the camera: the walls in front drop down and the ones behind stand up.
- [ ] At night the lamps glow and light the room; switch one off with a second tap.
- [ ] Catalogue, storage, rooms (switch, rename, make bigger) and saved looks.
- [ ] Portrait and landscape: the sheets fit and nothing is hidden under the bars.

## Online (signed in)
- [ ] Visit a friend's farm: it looks up to date, even a big one.
- [ ] Help a friend at a busy workshop; send a gift; Ask a friend.
- [ ] Village Projects (level 20+): start one with a friend, give to it, see it finish.
- [ ] Leaderboards open and show sensible numbers.
- [ ] Phone notifications arrive (crops ready, a gift).

## Account lock (Josh only, on a test account)
- [ ] Lock the test account from the dashboard with a long reason. In portrait and landscape the notice fits, the
      countdown ticks and a long reason can be scrolled with a finger.
- [ ] Unlock it: the notice goes within 5 minutes (or on reopening).

## What to send back
For each problem: what you did, what you expected, what happened, phone model, graphics setting, portrait or
landscape. A screenshot or short screen recording helps most.
