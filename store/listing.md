# Google Play store listing - Cozy Acres

Copy each block into Play Console > **Grow users > Store presence > Main store listing** (and the other pages named
below). Character counts were checked; Play's limits are in brackets.

## App details

**App name** (30): `Cozy Acres` (10 characters)

Alternative if "Cozy Acres" is taken in search: `Cozy Acres: Farm & Village` (26 characters).

**Short description** (80):

```

```

(75 characters)

**Full description** (4000):

```
Welcome to Cozy Acres, a gentle 3D farming game you can play at your own pace. Plant your fields, swipe to harvest, raise happy animals and turn a little patch of land into the cozy village of your dreams.

Completely free. No ads. No in-app purchases. Every coin and gem is earned by playing.

GROW YOUR FARM
- Plant 18 kinds of crops, from wheat and carrots to strawberries, pumpkins and watermelons
- Swipe across your fields to plant or harvest lots at once, and chain big combos
- Grow apple, cherry, orange and other fruit trees
- Clear the wild land, buy new plots and watch your farm spread across the island

RAISE ANIMALS
- Look after chickens, cows, sheep, pigs and goats
- One tap collects eggs, milk and wool and feeds everyone again

MAKE TASTY GOODS
- Run a feed mill, bakery, sugar mill, dairy, loom and jam kitchen
- Bake bread and pies, churn butter and cheese, knit scarves and sweaters

FILL ORDERS AND GET RICH
- Deliver orders from the village, load the delivery truck and run your own roadside stall
- Trade with the travelling merchant when he comes by

DECORATE AND RELAX
- Place flowerbeds, lanterns, fountains, statues and more to raise your farm's Charm
- Watch golden sunsets, moonlit nights, fireflies and crops swaying in the wind
- Create your own farmer with ready-made avatars, colours, hats and pets

PLAY WITH FRIENDS (optional)
- Add friends with a friend code and send gifts with a short note
- Buy and sell goods with other farmers on the Shared Market
- Climb the leaderboards for level, farm value, Charm and this week's XP
- Sign in with Google to back up your farm and keep playing on your phone, tablet or computer

ALWAYS SOMETHING TO DO
- 50 levels with new crops, animals, buildings and decorations to unlock
- Daily and weekly quests, awards, daily rewards and mystery crates
- Seasonal festivals: Harvest Festival, Winter Wonderland, Spring Blossom and Summer Fair
- Fill your Collection Book with everything you discover
- Your farm keeps growing while you are away

Made with love by Josh Makes Games, a tiny indie studio. Questions or ideas? Email joshmakesgames92@gmail.com - we read every message.
```

**Category:** Games > **Simulation** (recommended). Farming and village builders sit in Simulation on Google
Play, next to the games players compare it with. Casual is the alternative, but Simulation fits the feature set better.
